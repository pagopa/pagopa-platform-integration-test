## Common patterns
The failures in the WISP integration test suite predominantly cluster into two major categories:
1. **Environment / Health-Check Failures**: Several tests fail instantly during pre-flight checks (`health-check systems or subscription-key errors`), pointing to intermittent availability or misconfigured subscription keys in the test environment.
2. **Missing Receipt Events (`receipt-ok` / `RT_SEND_SUCCESS`)**: A large group of test scenarios successfully proceed through checkout redirection, but fail when validating that the `receipt-ok` business process event with status `RT_SEND_SUCCESS` was emitted. This indicates a widespread issue in WISP receipt generation, dispatch, or event logging asynchronous pipelines.
