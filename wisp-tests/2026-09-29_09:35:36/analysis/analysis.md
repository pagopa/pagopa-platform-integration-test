## Common patterns
All 6 failures share the exact same root cause: the assertion `check_event(context, 'receipt-ok', 'status', 'RT_SEND_SUCCESS')` fails because no `receipt-ok` events with the `RT_SEND_SUCCESS` status are recorded during the WISP session check. This indicates a systemic issue in the environment or application where the final payment receipt notification or event emission pipeline is broken across various payment types (single RPT, multi-RPT, multi-beneficiary, and GPD-linked positions).
