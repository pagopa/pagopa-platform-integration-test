## Common patterns
All 6 failures across the WISP integration test suite share the exact same assertion failure: `AssertionError: There are not events with business process receipt-ok.` and fail at the same verification step (`check_wisp_session_timers_del_and_rts_were_sent`). This indicates a systematic issue affecting receipt generation (`receipt-ok`) and the subsequent sending of payment receipts (`RT_SEND_SUCCESS`) across various payment types (single payments, carts, with/without stamps, and existing GPD debt positions).
