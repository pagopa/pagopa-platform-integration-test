## Common patterns

1. **Unexpected Redirects to Root URL (`/`)**: Multiple navigation failures (#4, #7, #8) failed because the browser unexpectedly fell back to `https://uat.checkout.pagopa.it/` instead of maintaining session/route state, preventing the execution of back button interactions.
2. **Missing PSP Options in UAT**: Postepay PSP selection timed out in both guest (#2) and authenticated (#6) payment flows, pointing to an environment or PSP service loading delay/unavailability in UAT.
3. **Linear Route Mismatches**: Navigation flows (#3, #5) failed URL assertions due to navigation remaining stuck on `/inserisci-dati-avviso` instead of advancing to `/dati-pagamento`.
