## Common patterns

1. **Missing PSP in UAT Environment**: Tests #1 and #4 failed because `#psp-radio-PPAYITR1XXX` was missing on `/lista-psp`. This points to an environment configuration issue where the Postepay PSP is inactive or unreturned by the backend service.
