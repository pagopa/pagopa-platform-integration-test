## Common patterns

1. **Broken PSP List Sorting (Application Bug)**: Failures #1 and #8 indicate that PSP list sorting (both ascending and descending) on the summary page is non-functional or inverted.
2. **Unexpected Session/URL Redirects to Root (Application Bug)**: Failures #2 and #6 fail with timeouts looking for the 'Indietro' button because the application unexpectedly drops back to the root URL (`https://dev.checkout.pagopa.it/`).
