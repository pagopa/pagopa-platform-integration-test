## Common patterns

- **PSP Selection Timeout**: Tests 2 and 5 both failed with a 5000ms timeout attempting to locate `#psp-radio-PPAYITR1XXX` on `/lista-psp`. This points to a potential issue with Postepay availability or delayed rendering in the DEV environment's PSP list response.
