## Common patterns

- **PSP Selection Timeout (`#psp-radio-PPAYITR1XXX`)**: Failures #1 and #6 both fail due to a 5000 ms locator timeout attempting to select the Postepay PSP on `/lista-psp`. This points to an environment issue where the Postepay service is either disabled or responding too slowly.
- **Back Navigation Navigation/State Issues**: Failures #3 and #5 fail at the exact same step while trying to locate the `Indietro` button. In both cases, the browser remains at the base URL (`https://dev.checkout.pagopa.it/`) instead of being on the targeted sub-routes (`/scegli-metodo` or `/inserisci-carta`), indicating unexpected redirects or unhandled navigation flakiness.
