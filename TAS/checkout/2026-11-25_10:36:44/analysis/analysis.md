## Common patterns

- **PSP Selection Rendering Failures**: Failures #1 and #3 failed due to missing/unrenderable `#psp-radio-PPAYITR1XXX` elements on `/lista-psp`, indicating slow endpoint response times for fetching available payment service providers.
