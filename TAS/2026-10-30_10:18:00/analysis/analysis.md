## Common patterns
- **PSP Selection Timeouts**: Multiple failures (#3, #4, #9, #12) stem from timeouts locating the Postepay PSP radio button (`#psp-radio-PPAYITR1XXX`), indicating potential environment instability or slow loading of the PSP list page.
- **Back Navigation Failures**: Failures #2, #5, #7, and #8 consistently fail on locating the "Indietro" button, pointing to a regression or selector mismatch in the back navigation UI component.
- **Payment & Stub Failures**: Wordline payment completions and invalid notice code error validations failed due to unexpected error messages returned by the backend or creditor mocks.
